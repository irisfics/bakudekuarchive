Deathly Beautiful

by: KyuukaKoinu on ao3

Summary: Katsuki always believed the first emotion he felt about the mysterious man was anger. Unchecked, pure fucking resentment. He thought the bitter emotion coursed through him like a raging river and struck lightning on every nerve he had. But when he truly thought back on it—the first emotion he felt wasn’t anger. It wasn't even hate or mild dislike. It was morbid curiosity.
