Summary: In an alternate mirror reality resides a slightly different version of Izuku Midoriya and the rest of the cast of My Hero Academia. In a world that mirrors the canon universe albeit with a few changes.

The Butterfly Effect. A tiny butterfly flapping its wings today may lead to a devastating hurricane weeks from now.

Fate is a strange and fragile thing, events left up to chance can cause a ripple in the very fabric of time and life as we know it. With each simplistic choice we make a consequence is paid.

Öde is an alternate word for fate. Something that Izuku Midoriya has found himself constantly at odds with. Rather it's accepting a shitty hand at life or carving out his own path and finding a whole new destiny for himself. A simple chance encounter, a mere choice, and before you even have a chance to breathe, an alternate reality is born anew, with only yourself left to blame. Izuku realizes this very fact much too late.

His story starts with something as simple as a bite and ends with a trail of bodies and a body count no longer worth counting.
