About The Archive

created by irisfics


On October 29th, 2026, AO3 announced that the website would be down for 2-4 weeks. This caused a massive panic for readers. In order to combat this problem, many AO3 users began creating their own archives. However, with a mass influx of fics being added, there have been many organizational issues. I created this website to streamline bakudeku fics to readers so they wouldn't have to hunt for the fics they're looking for.
